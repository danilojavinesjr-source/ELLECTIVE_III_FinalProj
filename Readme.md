This Dream Dormitory System Has a tenant login and admin login, Tenants are able to monitor their monthly utilty bills 
such as electricity and water bill they are also able to view some information of their existing roomates and see 
the past payments or history of their payments.

Thge Admin login UI can See present pending clients and Assign rooms to them if there is available , there is also a tenants 
section where the admin can see the information of all tenants . The rooms section displys an interactive room capacity 
and who is assign in those rooms aswell as a part where the admin can input readings of bills to calculate and indicate to the tenants
The record section has a button to generate monthly rent and set tenats status to paid when they are clear for pending bills
